# Proposal

## Why

The portfolio is clear and recruiter-friendly, but its restrained presentation no longer reflects Karthik's ambition for the site or his interest in space. The next iteration should become an unusually polished, highly interactive cosmic experience while keeping every professional claim, section label, and action immediately understandable to recruiters.

## What Changes

- Replace the restrained ambient treatment with a layered space environment featuring richer star depth, nebula light, orbital geometry, occasional celestial events, and pointer-responsive effects on capable devices.
- Introduce purposeful cinematic motion across the hero, sections, cards, timeline, capabilities, portrait, and contact area without scroll hijacking or content gates.
- Elevate the terminal and WhatBroke demo into the primary interactive showpieces through dimensional presentation, reactive lighting, scanning, telemetry, and anomaly-analysis feedback.
- Add premium spatial treatments such as holographic borders, controlled 3D depth, parallax, illuminated timelines, constellation-like capability connections, and responsive surface highlights.
- Keep conventional recruiter terminology—Projects, Professional Experience, Skills, About, and Contact—and keep all hiring-critical content visible without interaction.
- Replace vague portfolio wording with résumé-grounded positioning around full-stack production software, backend systems, AWS, AI-assisted engineering, and availability in Canada.
- Align professional experience with the résumé's supported evidence, including AI-assisted workflows, 1 kHz ECG ingestion, load shedding, AWS deployments, and an internal platform used by approximately 1,000 faculty members.
- Add visible proof points for AWS certification, the cloud innovation hackathon result, production health-data work, and platform scale.
- Preserve responsive reflow, keyboard access, both themes, reduced-motion behavior, no-JavaScript content access, and adaptive performance limits.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `portfolio-presentation`: Expands the visual and interaction contract from restrained technical-editorial styling to an ambitious cosmic interface, and updates recruiter-facing copy and evidence to match the current résumé.

## Impact

- Primary implementation: `index.html`, `style.css`, `mediaqueries.css`, and `script.js`.
- Existing terminal, WhatBroke demo, project demos, portrait, themes, and canvas become integration points rather than being replaced by a framework.
- Metadata and structured profile content may change to remain consistent with the revised visible positioning.
- No new runtime service, build system, or third-party dependency is introduced; the site remains vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
- More advanced effects increase rendering complexity, so capability-aware animation tiers, visibility pausing, DPR limits, and reduced-motion fallbacks are required.
