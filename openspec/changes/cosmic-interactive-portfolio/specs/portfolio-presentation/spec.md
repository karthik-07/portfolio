## REMOVED Requirements

### Requirement: Singular signature interaction

**Reason**: The approved direction expands the signature beyond the terminal into a page-wide cosmic experience with multiple coordinated interaction moments.

**Migration**: Retain the terminal as a primary showpiece, but integrate it with the broader cosmic environment and keep all critical content outside it.

### Requirement: Restrained interaction model

**Reason**: The new direction explicitly calls for an ambitious interactive presentation rather than globally removing pointer response, tilt, and spatial effects.

**Migration**: Reintroduce only bounded, capability-aware effects that do not gate content, hijack scrolling, change control targets, or run under reduced motion.

### Requirement: Coherent technical-editorial visual system

**Reason**: The restrained editorial treatment is being replaced by a more expressive cosmic visual system.

**Migration**: Preserve readable Geist typography, selective JetBrains Mono, accessible sizing, theme parity, and strong hierarchy inside the new cosmic art direction.
